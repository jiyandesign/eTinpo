The Rise of eTINPO: Turning Restrictions into Innovation

Since 2022, the United States and the European Union have implemented regulatory measures targeting PFAS, with a complete ban on producing and selling PFAS-containing textiles set to take effect in 2025. As major manufacturers actively seek solutions, eTINPO has emerged as a pioneer in sustainable, functional textiles, ushering in a new era of eco-friendly innovation. Founded in 2023, eTINPO is young and energetic—our story is just beginning.
