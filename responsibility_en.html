RESPONSIBILITY

Integrity and Responsibility

Integrity and responsibility are core values in our partnerships. We prioritize honesty and transparency to build strong relationships. By offering innovative technology, we empower individuals and businesses to enhance efficiency while collaborating to inspire industries and support environmental protection.

Protecting the Environment

Prioritizing ecological sustainability is fundamental to our research and development efforts. Our goal is to minimize the environmental impact by implementing processes that reduce carbon emissions and promote a sustainable conception for future generations. Moreover, our focus extends beyond performance; it is also about aligning with future needs.

Focusing on Technology

We develop high-performance, low-carbon, waterproof, and breathable products to meet our customers' needs. To achieve this functionality, we utilize non-toxic, recyclable polypropylene materials free from PFOA and PFAS and employ processes with low carbon emissions.

Mission and Vision

Actively embodying our brand philosophy, we strive to withstand the test of time with our brand values. With clear goals, we aim to continuously innovate and improve beyond environmental considerations, breaking through existing frameworks to pave a new path in the textile industry.

Corporate Responsibility

We advocate for open, honest, kind, and collaborative communication environments, valuing all personnel's fair welfare and diverse culture. Actively embodying our corporate philosophy and business ethics, we strive to set industry benchmarks that help us give back to society.

OUR CORE VALUES

Reduce Carbon Footprint

We realize our vision by selecting pure materials, using a solvent-free process, enhancing energy efficiency, optimizing production workflows, and minimizing carbon emissions in transportation and packaging.

Prioritize Human Rights and Labor Practices

We are committed to ensuring that our employees are treated fairly and protected by fundamental rights. We provide a safe and healthy working environment, fair compensation, and reasonable working hours. Committed to sustainable development, we adhere to labor laws and international standards, promoting workplace equality and respect. Through these efforts, we aim to create a more responsible and socially valuable business model.

Emphasize on Corporate Social Responsibility

eTINPO is committed to sustainable development, focusing on reducing environmental pollution and promoting green manufacturing practices. We prioritize functionality and non-toxicity in our products while emphasizing eco-friendly and sustainable materials. By collaborating with our partners, we actively participate in community initiatives and support environmental protection programs. We are dedicated to creating a fair and safe working environment for all.

Transparency in Production Process

eTINPO promotes the circular economy by incorporating sustainability into our product design and production processes. We are committed to using clean materials and prioritizing recyclable and renewable resources to minimize environmental impact. Additionally, we emphasize product lifecycle management, which ensures that materials can be reclaimed and repurposed after use, enabling efficient resource circulation.

Sustainable and Non-Toxic from Start to Finish

eTINPO ensures that each stage meets environmental and safety standards to deliver safe and non-toxic products to consumers. We are dedicated to utilizing solvent-free, low-energy manufacturing technologies, completely eliminating the use and emission of harmful chemicals to ensure safety for both people and the environment.
